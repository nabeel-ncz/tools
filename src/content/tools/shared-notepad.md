Moving a link, a snippet, an address or a screenshot between your phone and laptop shouldn't mean emailing yourself. Shared Notepad gives each of your devices a permanent key and turns every device you add into a chat — type or paste text, drop in images, and it appears on the other side instantly. A tiny signaling server only helps the devices find each other; everything you send goes straight between them.
